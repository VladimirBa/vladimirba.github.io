Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by VON NAB E.V. ("we", "us", or "our") on the Google Play Store, including but not limited to Hex Tic Tac Toe: Fun & Strategy, Tic Tac Toe: Fun & Strategy, and any future games (collectively, the "Games" or "Apps").

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our Games, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. However, our Games use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service, which may automatically collect certain data to display ads and measure their performance.

The data collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • Device or Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP Address (which may be used to estimate general location)
  • App usage data (such as app launches, taps, and video views)
  • Diagnostic information (such as app performance and crash data)

For more details on what Google AdMob collects, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob Data Disclosure page[reference:0].

2. How Information Is Used

The data collected through Google AdMob is used for the following purposes:

  • To display advertisements (both personalized and non-personalized)
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud
  • To analyze app usage and improve the gaming experience

Ad personalization depends on your region and your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data collected by Google AdMob is shared with Google and its advertising partners in accordance with Google's policies[reference:1].

4. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Any data collected by Google AdMob is retained and handled by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies and retention schedules.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Games are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through our Games, please contact us so we can take appropriate action[reference:2].

6. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data directly, these requests should be directed to Google AdMob. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
